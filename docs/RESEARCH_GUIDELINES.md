# OpenMicroDuck Research Guidelines

This compatibility entry remains at the original path so existing links do not break.

- [English research guidelines](en/research-guidelines.md)
- [简体中文研究规范](zh-CN/research-guidelines.md)

The public-repository rule is simple: document public, attributable, reproducible Microduck research and clearly distinguish official facts from observation, inference, and assumptions. Confidential, leaked, private, unrelated proprietary, or otherwise non-public engineering information must not be added to this repository.
