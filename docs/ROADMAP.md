# OpenMicroDuck Public Documentation Roadmap

[English documentation](en/README.md) | [简体中文文档](zh-CN/README.md)

This roadmap tracks the **public research/documentation project**. It is not a product-development plan and does not describe any private hardware program.

## Current priorities

1. Maintain a sourced public Microduck hardware inventory without mislabeling it as an official BOM.
2. Track changes between official product specifications, source-code development hardware, and simulation assets.
3. Document the public mechanical/kinematic structure and preserve model-vs-production caveats.
4. Maintain a curated index of public reverse-engineering, simulator, RL-port, and interoperability projects.
5. Improve source-level citations down to commit/file paths where values are version-sensitive.
6. Add reproducible public measurements when contributors have access to production hardware.
7. Expand translations while keeping technical claims consistent across languages.
8. Keep provenance and licensing boundaries clear for upstream software, 3D assets, and community derivatives.

## Documentation areas

- Product/public specifications
- Hardware inventory and BOM status
- Mechanical structure and kinematics
- Electronics, buses, sensors, and power
- Runtime/software architecture
- Simulation, RL, and sim-to-real
- Community reverse engineering and tooling
- Evidence/provenance/licensing

## Contribution principle

New entries should be publicly attributable and suitable for an open repository. Private, confidential, leaked, unrelated proprietary, or otherwise non-public engineering information is outside the scope of OpenMicroDuck.
