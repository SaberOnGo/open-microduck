# OpenMicroDuck Public Documentation Roadmap

**English** | [简体中文](../zh-CN/roadmap.md)

This roadmap tracks the **public research and documentation project**. It is not a product-development plan and does not describe private hardware programs.

## Current priorities

1. Maintain a source-backed public Microduck hardware inventory without mislabeling it as an official BOM.
2. Keep concrete part numbers visible whenever official public evidence supports them.
3. Track differences between official product specifications, current source-tree development hardware, and simulation assets.
4. Document public mechanical/kinematic structure with clear model-vs-production caveats.
5. Maintain a reviewed index of reverse-engineering, simulator, RL-port, and interoperability projects.
6. Improve citations down to commit/file paths when values are version-sensitive.
7. Add reproducible public measurements from production hardware when available.
8. Keep English and Simplified Chinese documentation trees aligned topic-by-topic.
9. Preserve provenance and licensing boundaries for upstream software, 3D assets, and community derivatives.

## Documentation areas

- product/public specifications;
- hardware inventory and BOM status;
- mechanical structure and kinematics;
- electronics, buses, sensors, and power;
- runtime/software architecture;
- simulation, RL, and sim-to-real;
- community reverse engineering and tooling;
- evidence, provenance, and licensing.

## Contribution principle

New entries should be publicly attributable and suitable for an open repository. Private, confidential, leaked, unrelated proprietary, or otherwise non-public engineering information is outside the scope of OpenMicroDuck.

See the [research guidelines](research-guidelines.md).
